# Duo Status Bar 1.4.3-beta.1 — pre-release

Integrated with **Punch Hole Download Progress**: download progress, completion and clear now draw on the
Duo ring.

- Ring colour no longer flickers black/white on some ROMs.
- When the module is not running, the app says why and offers **Open LSPosed**, **Restart System UI**,
  **Export my log** and **Contact on Telegram**.
- Reports always carry the app, device, ROM, System UI and LSPosed state, and never send a previous run's
  dump. Bug reports stay disabled until the module is running.
